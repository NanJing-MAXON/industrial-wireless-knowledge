# What Do Ex db IIB T6 Gb and Ex db IIC T6 Gb Mean?

`Ex db IIB T6 Gb` and `Ex db IIC T6 Gb` are hazardous-area markings for flameproof equipment intended for gas atmospheres. They describe a protection concept, gas group, temperature class, and Equipment Protection Level. Suitability must be verified from the complete marking, exact certificate, schedule, ambient range, special conditions, and installed configuration.

## Marking Breakdown

| Element | Engineering meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `Ex` | Equipment uses an explosion-protection concept |
| `db` | Flameproof enclosure with protection level `b` |
| `IIB` / `IIC` | Gas subgroup covered by the marked equipment |
| `T6` | Temperature class; verify the certified ambient and operating conditions |
| `Gb` | Equipment Protection Level for gas atmospheres |

IIC represents a more demanding gas subgroup than IIB, but an IIC marking does not remove the need to check every other part of the classification and certificate. Procurement should not compare products from the gas-group letters alone.

## Exact-Model Verification

- Confirm the installation country and accepted certification scheme.
- Obtain the site's Zone or Division, gas or vapour group, temperature class, Equipment Protection Level, and ambient range.
- Match the exact model, revision, and nameplate marking character by character.
- Review the certificate schedule and any special conditions of use.
- Verify enclosure material, flame paths, covers, fasteners, cable entries, glands, antennas, seals, mounting, earthing, and approved accessories.
- Confirm inspection, opening, isolation, repair, and maintenance procedures.

An IP rating addresses ingress protection and does not by itself establish flameproof protection. Likewise, a family name or visually similar enclosure is not evidence that every variant carries the same approval. Never modify a flame path, drill an enclosure, substitute a gland, change an antenna arrangement, or replace safety-critical hardware without controlled review.

## Purchase Documentation

Retain the current certificate and schedule, datasheet, installation manual, nameplate details, approved accessory list, inspection plan, and revision history for the supplied model. If a document is missing or values conflict, stop approval and request the latest controlled source.
